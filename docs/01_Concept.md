# EQ Concept

EQ (Equilibrium) is not a trading strategy.

EQ is a market structure model.

The purpose of the Engine is to identify areas where price is more likely to react.

Trading decisions are made by a separate Strategy module.

The philosophy is:

Engine != Strategy

The Engine should detect every valid level objectively.

The Strategy decides which levels are tradable.

This separation makes research easier and prevents logic duplication.
